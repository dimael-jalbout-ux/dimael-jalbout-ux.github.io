Model limits: constant annual savings, no taxes, grants, financing, inflation, degradation, residual value, carbon pricing or changes in energy prices. This is a learning demo, not investment advice or a forecast.

Why this model?

Investment conversations often mix assumptions and conclusions. The tool keeps the key inputs visible, gives a quick way to explore sensitivities, and makes clear which assumptions drive the result. In professional work, I used financial models to compare decarbonisation options for commercial property clients.
