Responsible-use notes: no personal or company data; no live model or external API; the labels below are hand-authored examples for interface testing. A production system would need privacy review, quality evaluation and a human review path for uncertain output.

Product choices

The core idea is not to automate a team's judgment. It is to reduce the effort needed to see recurring patterns, keep the evidence close to each suggested theme, and make uncertainty visible.

  • One clear review queue for low-confidence or high-impact cases.
  • Theme and text search for quick exploration.
  • Human confirmation before any operational decision.
  • Success measures are hypotheses to test, not achieved results: adoption, classification quality, analyst usefulness and time saved.

The earlier concept deck used simulated targets for evaluation. Those figures are not production results.
