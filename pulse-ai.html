METHOD NOTE

All scenarios, ratings and workflow descriptions are illustrative, hand-authored examples. The score is a transparent prioritisation aid, not an AI prediction, investment recommendation, or validated business case. No company data or live model is used.

HOW I THINK ABOUT ADOPTION

People stay
in control.

This concept frames AI adoption as an operations decision. A team first makes the workflow, pain point and current baseline explicit. Then it weighs upside against process readiness, control requirements and the cost of handling exceptions.

  • Define the unit of work: where does time, rework or delay occur?
  • Set a human boundary: which decisions must remain reviewable or require approval?
  • Prove the case: test quality, exception rates, cycle time and ownership with a small pilot.

The scenarios on this page are personal concept work. They do not describe a deployed system or client engagement.
