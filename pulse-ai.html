METHOD NOTE

All titles, details, labels and scores are fictional, hand-authored examples for exploring an interface. The review threshold is an illustrative rule, not a validated model. Filters and review actions run locally and are not saved.

THE PRODUCT QUESTION

Where should a team's attention go first?

The concept keeps three things together: a repeat signal, the original case details and a visible path for uncertainty. A team could use that structure to choose what to investigate, while keeping the operational decision with a person.

01 Patterns are a prompt02 Evidence stays inspectable03 Uncertainty gets a reviewer

What to test next: label quality, time-to-triage, usefulness to support leads, and whether a surfaced theme leads to a verified product fix. These are evaluation questions, not achieved outcomes.
